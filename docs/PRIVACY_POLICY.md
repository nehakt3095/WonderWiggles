# Privacy Policy — WonderWiggles: The Little Puzzle Planet

Effective Date: 2026-11-24

WonderWiggles is built specifically for young children aged 4–7 and their families.

## 1. Zero Personal Information Collected
WonderWiggles does not ask for, collect, store, or transmit names, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, device identifiers, or location data.

## 2. Local-Only Progress Storage
Completed levels, earned stars, character selection, and audio preferences are stored strictly locally on the device (`localStorage` on web, `Application.persistentDataPath` in Unity). Parents can clear all saved progress at any time inside the Parent Zone.

## 3. Zero Ads & Zero Third-Party Tracking
WonderWiggles contains no advertisements, no behavioral analytics, no social media links, and no chat features.

## 4. Parent-Gated Unlocks
Optional premium upgrades (unlocking Worlds 2–5 and companion characters) are protected behind a grown-up verification gate and processed solely through platform-approved billing (Google Play Billing on Android; Test Mode preview on web).
